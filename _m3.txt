test(api): bring four more modules under strict pyrefly

Graduate `test_workflow_draft_variable_service`, the human-input e2e form
service, `workflow_as_tool/test_tool` and the chat app generator.

The fakes in the two generator/tool tests now extend the real classes and skip
`super().__init__()`, which keeps them off redis and pydantic validation while
satisfying the declared types. Where the tests deliberately store `None` in a
non-optional mapped column, they use SQLAlchemy's `set_committed_value`, which
is the documented way to set an attribute without marking it dirty and avoids
the typed descriptor.
