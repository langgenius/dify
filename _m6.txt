test(api): bring four more modules under strict pyrefly

Graduate `test_structured_output`, `test_cot_completion_agent_runner`,
`hit_service` and `code_node_spec` out of `project-excludes` by removing the
suppressions they needed.

Two changes reach outside the tests because the production annotation was the
reason the suppressions were needed:

- `remove_additional_properties` was declared `schema: dict[str, Any]` while its
  first statement is `if not isinstance(schema, dict): return`. Under that
  annotation the guard is dead code, so the signature now says `object` and
  matches what the body has always accepted. The three production call sites
  pass mappings and are unaffected.
- the fake model instance in `test_structured_output` now mirrors the three
  `@overload`s of `ModelInstance.invoke_llm` instead of collapsing them into one
  `**kwargs` signature.

The remaining work is annotations, real production classes in place of local
stand-ins, and `CodeNode.init_node_data` instead of assigning `_node_data`
directly.
